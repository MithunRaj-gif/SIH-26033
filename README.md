# 1. PROPOSED SOLUTION

## 1.1 Introduction

Agriculture plays an important role in India's economy. However, farmers often face difficulties in receiving a fair price for their products. Agricultural products usually pass through several intermediaries, such as local traders, wholesalers, distributors and retailers, before reaching consumers.

Each intermediary adds additional costs to the product. As a result, farmers receive a smaller portion of the final selling price, while consumers are forced to pay more.

To solve this problem, we propose **FARMLINK – Direct Farm Connect**, a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants and bulk buyers.

The platform aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries through direct communication, collective selling, demand prediction and shared transportation facilities.

## 1.2 How FARMLINK Works

FARMLINK provides a common platform for farmers and buyers.

1. **Farmer Registration:** Farmers can register using their mobile number and provide basic details about their farms and location.

2. **Product Listing:** Farmers can upload details of available products, including quantity, expected price, harvest date and product images.

3. **Direct Buyer Connection:** Consumers, shops, supermarkets, hotels and restaurants can browse available products and place orders directly.

4. **Collective Selling:** Small farmers in the same area can combine their produce to fulfil larger orders. This helps them reach bulk buyers without depending on multiple traders.

5. **Demand Forecasting:** An AI-based system analyses previous orders and seasonal demand to estimate which products may be required in the coming days.

6. **Shared Logistics:** The platform groups nearby orders and arranges common pickup and delivery services to reduce transportation expenses.

7. **Transparent Payments:** Buyers can make digital payments, and farmers receive their agreed amount with a clear transaction record.

## 1.3 Unique Features and Innovation

FARMLINK introduces the following features:

- **Collective Harvest Marketplace:** Farmers can combine their available produce and sell it as a single bulk order.
- **Demand-Based Farming Insights:** AI analyses previous buying patterns and provides demand estimates to help farmers plan future production.
- **Smart Order Grouping:** Orders from nearby customers are combined to reduce delivery costs.
- **Transparent Price Breakdown:** Buyers can see the farmer's price, transportation cost and platform service charge separately.
- **Digital Farmer Scorecard:** Farmers can view their sales, earnings, completed orders and customer feedback.
- **Multilingual Interface:** The application supports regional languages such as Tamil, Telugu, Hindi and English.
- **Offline Order Assistance:** Farmers with limited internet access can receive assistance through a local collection centre or FPO representative.

The main innovation is the combination of collective selling, demand-based planning and shared logistics in one platform.

## 2.2 System Working Process

**Step 1 – Registration:** Farmers and buyers create accounts on the platform.

**Step 2 – Product Upload:** Farmers enter product details, available quantities and expected prices.

**Step 3 – Product Discovery:** Buyers search for products based on location, price and availability.

**Step 4 – Order Collection:** The system collects customer orders and identifies nearby farmers who can fulfil them.

**Step 5 – AI Demand Analysis:** The system analyses historical orders, seasonal trends and available stock to estimate demand.

**Step 6 – Logistics Management:** Nearby orders are grouped, and suitable pickup and delivery arrangements are suggested.

**Step 7 – Payment and Delivery:** The buyer completes payment, the order is delivered and the farmer receives the agreed payment.
